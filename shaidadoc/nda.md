## 3. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including but not limited to:

### 3.1 Technical Information
- Source code, algorithms, and software architecture
- AI models, training data, and machine learning processes
- API documentation and technical specifications
- Database schemas and system designs
- Security protocols and authentication methods

### 3.2 Business Information
- Business plans, strategies, and financial projections
- Customer lists, pricing, and contract terms
- Marketing plans and go-to-market strategies
- Product roadmaps and feature specifications
- Partnership agreements and vendor relationships

### 3.3 Other Protected Information
- Trade secrets and know-how
- Research and development activities
- Employee and team information
- Unpublished patent applications
- Any information marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary"

### 3.4 Exclusions
Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Recipient
- Was already known to Recipient prior to disclosure
- Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with notice)

## 4. Obligations of Recipient

### 4.1 Duty of Confidentiality
Recipient agrees to:
- Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence
- Use at least the same degree of care as for Recipient's own confidential information (minimum: reasonable care)
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third parties
- Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose stated in Section 2

### 4.2 Permitted Disclosures
Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to:
- Employees, contractors, and advisors who **need to know** for the Purpose
- Such persons are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement
- Recipient remains liable for any breaches by such persons

### 4.3 Security Measures
Recipient shall:
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Store Confidential Information securely (encrypted where applicable)
- Limit access to authorized personnel only
- Report any suspected or actual unauthorized disclosure immediately
