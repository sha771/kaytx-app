| Feature | Tone | Style |
|---------|------|-------|
| Unified Inbox | Efficient, organized | Clean, crisp |

### Recording Tips

1. **Hydration:** Drink water 30 minutes before recording
2. **Warm-up:** Do vocal exercises (lip trills, humming)
3. **Environment:** Record in a quiet, echo-free room
4. **Microphone:** Use a quality USB microphone
5. **Position:** Maintain 6-8 inches from microphone
6. **Pacing:** Vary speed for emphasis
7. **Natural:** Sound conversational, not robotic

```
Font: Inter or SF Pro
Size: 48-64px headlines, 24-32px body
Color: White with subtle drop shadow
Animation: Fade in + slight upward motion (0.5s)
```
