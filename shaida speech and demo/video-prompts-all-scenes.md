## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS (All Scenes)

### Format Requirements:
- **Resolution:** 4K UHD (3840x2160) primary delivery, 2K (2560x1440) web, 1080p proxies
- **Aspect Ratios:** 
  - 16:9 (1.78:1) — Primary cinematic version
  - 1:1 Square — Instagram feed/ads (crop center 70%)
  - 9:16 Vertical — TikTok/Reels/Stories (crop center 50% width)
  - 4:5 — Instagram portrait feed
- **Frame Rate:** 60fps for UI animations (deliver 24fps cinematic version for film festivals)
- **Color Space:** Rec. 709 (broadcast), HDR10+ (premium), P3-D65 (theatrical)
- **Codec:** ProRes 422 HQ (master), H.265 HEVC (delivery), H.264 (web)

### Cinematic Language:
- **Lens Choices:** 
  - Wide shots: 18-24mm for scale and immersion
  - Medium shots: 35-50mm for natural perspective
  - Details: 85-100mm macro for UI elements
- **Camera Movement:** 
  - Dolly: Smooth linear movements (0.3m/s average)
  - Orbit: 360° around subjects at 15°/sec
  - Push-in: Slow intensification (0.5m over 3s)
  - Pan: Fluid across UI panels (2-4s duration)
- **Depth of Field:** 
  - Shallow (f/1.4-f/2.8) for subject isolation
  - Deep (f/8-f/11) for complex UI layouts

### Safe Zones:
- **16:9 Title Safe:** 10% margin all sides
- **1:1 Square:** Keep critical UI within 1080x1080 center crop
- **9:16 Vertical:** Essential elements within 540px horizontal center
- **Captions:** Lower third 15% reserved for subtitle burn-in
- **Action Safe:** Keep motion graphics within 90% frame boundary

### Brand Elements (Consistent Across All Scenes):
- **Primary Navy:** #0B1120 (deep space backgrounds)
- **Accent Cyan:** #00D9FF (AI intelligence, technology)
- **Accent Purple:** #7B61FF (creativity, innovation)
- **Success Gold:** #FFB800 (achievements, metrics, ROI)
- **Alert Red:** #FF4757 (urgency, errors, warnings)
- **Human Warmth:** #FFB347, #FF9500 (human team elements)
- **Typography:** 
  - Display: SF Pro Display Bold (60-120px headlines)
  - UI: Inter SemiBold (24-48px interface elements)
  - Data: JetBrains Mono (metrics, numbers)
  - Subtitles: Inter Regular with 120% line height

### Animation Timing Standards:
- **UI Stagger:** 0.15s between sequential elements
- **Counter Duration:** 
  - <1,000: 1.5s with easing
  - 1,000-10,000: 2.0s with motion blur
  - >10,000: 2.5s with segmented counting
- **Camera Movement:** 
  - Ease-in-out-expo for dramatic moves
  - Linear for continuous orbits
  - Smooth dampening for stops
- **Transitions:** 
  - Cross-dissolve: 0.5s standard
  - Motion wipe: 0.8s match-cut
  - Shockwave: 0.3s impact frames
- **Particle Density:** 
  - Low: 25-50 (minimal scenes)
  - Medium: 100-200 (standard UI)
  - High: 500+ (AI hierarchy reveal)

### VFX Requirements:
- **Volumetric Lighting:** God rays, dust particles, atmospheric depth
- **Lens Effects:** Anamorphic flares (2.39:1 moments), chromatic aberration (subtle), lens dust
- **Particle Systems:** 
  - Data streams: 10,000+ particles with turbulence
  - Connection lines: Bezier curves with glow
  - Success bursts: Radial explosion from center
- **Shader Effects:** 
  - Glass-morphism: Blur 20px + saturation 120% + white 10% overlay
  - Holographic: Scan lines + chromatic shift + flicker
  - Energy: Fractal noise + time displacement
