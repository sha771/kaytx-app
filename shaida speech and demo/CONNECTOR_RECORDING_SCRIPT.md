## Recording Tips

### Voice Preparation

1. **Hydration:** Drink water 30 minutes before recording
2. **Warm-up:** Do vocal exercises (lip trills, humming)
3. **Environment:** Record in a quiet, echo-free room
4. **Microphone:** Use a quality USB microphone or professional setup
5. **Position:** Maintain consistent distance from microphone (6-8 inches)

### Delivery Style

- **Confidence:** Speak with authority and conviction
- **Energy:** Maintain enthusiasm throughout
- **Clarity:** Enunciate clearly, avoid mumbling
- **Pacing:** Vary speed for emphasis, avoid monotone
- **Natural:** Sound conversational, not robotic

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Don't rush through the script
- Don't use filler words (um, uh, like)
- Don't sound scripted or robotic
- Don't vary volume too dramatically
- Don't breathe loudly into the microphone

### Post-Recording

- Listen to the full recording
- Note sections that need re-recording
- Edit out mistakes and long pauses
- Add background music (low volume)
- Normalize audio levels
- Export in high-quality format (WAV or high-bitrate MP3)
