## Project Overview

**Company Brain** is an AI-powered institutional knowledge system that automatically captures, organizes, and preserves company intelligence. It serves as the central nervous system of an organization, ensuring that critical knowledge never walks out the door when employees leave.

**Core Value Proposition:**
- Never lose institutional knowledge again
- Reduce onboarding time by 60%
- Protect against employee turnover impact
- Make company wisdom searchable and actionable

- Zoom: 75%
- Device mode: Responsive

# Recording software
- OBS Studio (free) or Camtasia (paid)
- Capture: Browser window
- Frame rate: 60fps
- Output: MKV (remux to MP4)

## Recording Tips

1. **Smooth scrolling** - Enable smooth scroll in browser settings
2. **Click highlighting** - Use OBS click effects or Camtasia cursor effects
3. **Timing** - Practice the flow before recording
4. **Clean environment** - Close all other apps, hide bookmarks
5. **Consistent zoom** - Keep zoom at 75% throughout
