## Production Notes

### Voice Style
- Confident, visionary, energetic
- Pace: Moderate with strategic pauses
- Tone: Professional yet approachable
- Emphasis: Human-AI collaboration benefits

### Visual Style
- Clean, modern, minimal
- Smooth transitions between scenes
- Color palette: Blue, purple, green (KAYTX brand colors)
- Typography: Bold, readable, consistent
- Highlight AI agents with purple badges to distinguish from humans

### Music
- Upbeat, modern tech background music
- Volume: Low, doesn't compete with voiceover
- Mood: Inspiring, collaborative, forward-thinking

### Graphics
- Animated icons for each feature
- Progress bars and circular indicators
- Color-coded badges and status indicators
- Smooth data visualizations
- Purple accent for AI-related elements
