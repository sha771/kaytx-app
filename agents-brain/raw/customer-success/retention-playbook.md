# Customer Success Knowledge Base

## Customer Lifecycle Stages

### 1. Onboarding (Day 0–30)
**Goal**: Time-to-first-value under 7 days
- Welcome email within 1 hour of signup
- Kickoff call scheduled within 3 business days
- Implementation plan documented in shared workspace
- Success criteria agreed upon and tracked

**Health Signals**:
- ✅ Green: Logged in 3+ times in week 1, completed setup checklist
- ⚠️ Yellow: Logged in 1-2 times, partial setup
- 🔴 Red: No login in 7 days → trigger re-engagement sequence

### 2. Adoption (Day 30–90)
**Goal**: Weekly active usage + feature expansion
- Bi-weekly check-ins for enterprise tier
- Monthly usage reports sent to stakeholders
- Identify and remove friction points via session analysis
- Promote advanced features based on usage patterns

### 3. Expansion (Day 90+)
**Goal**: Increase NRR through upsells and seat expansion
- Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) for accounts >$50K ARR
- Identify expansion signals: usage near plan limits, new team requests
- Cross-sell adjacent products when relevant

### 4. Renewal (90 days before renewal)
**Goal**: 95%+ gross renewal rate
- Renewal forecast meeting at T-90 days
- Executive sponsor engagement at T-60 days
- Contract sent at T-30 days
- Auto-renewal confirmation at T-7 days

## Churn Prevention

### Early Warning Indicators
| Signal | Risk Level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Login frequency dropped 50%+ | High | CSM outreach within 48h |
| Support tickets trending negative | High | Escalate to CSM + Product |
| Champion left the company | High | Multi-thread + identify new champion |
| Usage below activation threshold | Medium | Targeted re-engagement campaign |
| Invoice disputed | Medium | Finance + CSM joint outreach |

### Win-Back Protocol
1. Exit interview within 5 days of churn
2. Document reason in CRM
3. Add to win-back campaign list
4. Re-engage at 30, 60, 90 days with relevant updates
5. Offer incentive at 60 days if applicable

## CS Metrics & KPIs

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Gross Retention Rate | ≥95% | Monthly |
| Net Retention Rate | ≥110% | Monthly |
| Net Promoter Score | ≥50 | Quarterly |
| Time to First Value | ≤7 days | Per onboarding |
| CSAT (post-interaction) | ≥4.5/5 | Per ticket |
| Expansion Rate | ≥15% annually | Quarterly |

## Escalation Paths

### Product Issues
- Bug impacting multiple customers → P1 ticket + Slack #cs-escalations
- Feature gap blocking renewal → Product Manager within 24h
- Data loss concern → Engineering + Legal immediately

### Billing Disputes
- Under $1,000 → CSM authority to credit
- $1,000–$10,000 → CS Manager approval
- Over $10,000 → VP CS + CFO approval
