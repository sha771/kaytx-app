# HR Policies and Procedures

## Employee Handbook Overview

This document outlines our company policies, procedures, and expectations for all employees. These policies ensure a fair, safe, and productive work environment.

## Employment Policies

### Equal Employment Opportunity

We are committed to providing equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, national origin, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

### Anti-Harassment Policy

**Zero Tolerance**: We have zero tolerance for harassment of any kind, including but not limited to:
- Sexual harassment
- Racial harassment
- Age-based harassment
- Disability harassment
- Any other form of discrimination

**Reporting Procedure**:
1. Report incident to HR immediately
2. HR will conduct confidential investigation
3. Appropriate action will be taken
4. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited

### Attendance and Punctuality

**Work Hours**: Standard business hours are 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

**Reporting Late**: Employees arriving more than 15 minutes late must notify their supervisor.

**Absence Policy**:
- Notify supervisor at least 2 hours before shift start
- Provide reason for absence
- Excessive absences may lead to disciplinary action

## Compensation and Benefits

### Salary Structure

Salaries are reviewed annually based on:
- Performance evaluations
- Market conditions
- Company financial performance
- Employee tenure and contributions

### Pay Schedule

Employees are paid on a monthly basis on the last business day of each month.

### Benefits Package

**Health Insurance**:
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Company covers 80% of premiums
- Coverage begins after 30 days of employment

**Retirement Plan**:
- 401(k) with company match up to 4%
- Vesting schedule: 20% per year, fully vested after 5 years
- Eligible after 90 days of employment

**Paid Time Off**:
- Vacation: 15 days per year (accrues monthly)
- Sick leave: 10 days per year
- Personal days: 3 days per year
- Holidays: 10 paid holidays per year

**Other Benefits**:
- Life insurance (1x salary)
- Disability insurance
- Employee assistance program
- Professional development allowance
- Gym membership reimbursement

## Performance Management

### Performance Reviews

**Annual Reviews**: Conducted in December for all employees.

**Quarterly Check-ins**: Brief meetings to discuss progress and goals.

**Self-Evaluation**: Employees complete self-evaluation before annual review.

### Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

If performance doesn't meet expectations:
1. Written warning with specific improvement areas
2. 30-day period to demonstrate improvement
3. Weekly check-ins with manager
4. Final evaluation at end of period

### Promotion Criteria

Promotions are based on:
- Consistent high performance
- Leadership potential
- Company need
- Professional development
- Tenure in current role

## Workplace Policies

### Remote Work Policy

**Eligibility**: Employees with 6+ months tenure and proven performance may request remote work.

**Requirements**:
- Reliable internet connection
- Dedicated workspace
- Available during core hours (10 AM - 3 PM)
- Regular communication with team

**Approval Process**: Submit request to manager with justification. HR makes final decision.

### Code of Conduct

**Professional Behavior**:
- Treat all colleagues with respect
- Maintain confidentiality
- Use company resources responsibly
- Represent company professionally

**Social Media**:
- Don't share confidential information
- Be respectful in online interactions
- Clarify personal opinions vs. company positions
- Report social media issues to HR

### Drug and Alcohol Policy

**Workplace**: No alcohol or illegal drugs allowed on company premises.

**Business Functions**: Alcohol may be served at company events with manager approval.

**Testing**: Drug testing may be required for safety-sensitive positions or if reasonable suspicion exists.

## Leave Policies

### Medical Leave

**Short-term**: Use sick leave for illnesses under 5 days.

**Long-term**: Apply for FMLA or disability leave for extended illnesses.

**Documentation**: Medical certification required for absences over 3 days.

### Family Leave

**Maternity/Paternity**: Up to 12 weeks unpaid leave under FMLA.

**Bereavement**: 3 days paid leave for immediate family members.

**Caregiver**: Up to 5 days paid leave per year for caring for sick family members.

### Military Leave

Employees called to active duty are entitled to unpaid leave with job protection.

## Training and Development

### Onboarding Program

**First Week**:
- Company orientation
- Department introduction
- System and tool training
- Meet with team members

**First Month**:
- Role-specific training
- Shadow senior colleagues
- Complete compliance training
- Set initial goals with manager

### Ongoing Training

**Mandatory Training**:
- Annual compliance training
- Security awareness training
- Anti-harassment training

**Professional Development**:
- $2,000 annual allowance for courses and certifications
- Internal training programs
- Conference attendance (with approval)
- Tuition reimbursement for job-related education

## Safety and Security

### Workplace Safety

**Emergency Procedures**:
- Fire drills conducted quarterly
- Emergency exits clearly marked
- First aid kits available on each floor
- AEDs located in common areas

**Reporting Hazards**: Report any safety concerns to facilities or HR immediately.

### Data Security

**Password Requirements**:
- Minimum 12 characters
- Mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
- Change every 90 days
- No sharing passwords

**Data Handling**:
- Lock computer when away from desk
- Use VPN for remote access
- Report security incidents immediately
- Don't store company data on personal devices

## Disciplinary Procedures

### Progressive Discipline

**Step 1**: Verbal warning (documented)

**Step 2**: Written warning

**Step 3**: Final written warning

**Step 4**: Suspension or termination

**Immediate Termination**: For serious violations (theft, violence, harassment, etc.)

### Grievance Process

Employees may file grievances regarding:
- Unfair treatment
- Policy violations
- Working conditions

**Process**:
1. Submit written grievance to HR
2. HR investigates within 5 business days
3. Meeting with employee and relevant parties
4. Resolution provided within 10 business days
5. Appeal process available

## Separation Policies

### Resignation

**Notice Period**: 2 weeks notice required for professional positions, 4 weeks for management.

**Exit Process**:
- Submit resignation letter to manager and HR
- Complete exit interview
- Return company property
- Hand over work responsibilities
- Receive final paycheck and benefits information

### Termination

**For Cause**: Immediate termination for serious policy violations.

**Layoffs**: 2 weeks notice or 2 weeks pay in lieu of notice.

**Severance**: Based on tenure and position, determined on case-by-case basis.

## Conclusion

These policies are designed to create a positive, productive work environment. All employees are expected to familiarize themselves with these policies and adhere to them. Questions should be directed to HR.
