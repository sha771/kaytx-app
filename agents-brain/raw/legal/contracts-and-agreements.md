# Legal Department Knowledge Base

## Contract Lifecycle Management

### Standard Contract Templates
- **NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement)**: Use for all external partnerships. Standard term: 3 years.
- **MSA (Master Services Agreement)**: Required for vendor relationships exceeding $10,000/year.
- **SOW (Statement of Work)**: Must reference parent MSA. Include deliverables, timeline, payment terms.
- **DPA (Data Processing Agreement)**: Required for all vendors handling PII per GDPR Article 28.

### Approval Thresholds
| Contract Value | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | Department Head |
| $5,000 – $50,000 | VP + Legal |
| $50,000 – $250,000 | CFO + General Counsel |
| Over $250,000 | CEO + Board Approval |

## Compliance Frameworks

### GDPR (EU)
- Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR): Must respond within 30 days
- Right to Erasure: Verify backup deletion across all systems
- Breach Notification: 72-hour window to authorities

### CCPA/CPRA (California)
- "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link required on website
- Annual privacy policy update by January 1st
- Consumer request response: 45 days

### SOC 2 Type II
- Annual audit window: Q1
- Required controls: CC1-CC9 (Common Criteria)
- Continuous monitoring via Drata or Vanta

## IP Management
- All employee-created work product is work-for-hire per employment agreement
- Patent applications: File within 12 months of public disclosure
- Trademark renewals: Track via USPTO docket, file Section 8 at year 5-6

## Litigation Hold Protocol
1. Legal issues hold notice to IT
2. IT suspends all auto-deletion for affected custodians
3. Data preserved in isolated legal hold vault
4. Quarterly review of active holds
5. Release only upon written Legal authorization
